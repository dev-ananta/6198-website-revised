LOBSTER MACHINE / ENGINEER. BUILD. COMPETE.
FTC TEAM 6198 / ENGINEERING

DESIGNED TO
PERFORM.

Every robot starts with a problem to solve. Our team plans, designs, builds, programs, and tests mechanisms to create a robot that works reliably under competition conditions.

01 / MECHANICAL

Design & Build

We explore mechanisms, create CAD models, choose materials, and build prototypes. Testing helps us identify weak points and make the design more dependable.

02 / SOFTWARE

Programming

Our programming includes driver-controlled movement, motor control, sensors, and autonomous routines. We test code repeatedly to make the robot's behavior more consistent.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Testing & Iteration

We test speed, accuracy, repeatability, and reliability, then use what we learn to decide what to improve next.

04 / STRATEGY

Competition Ready

We consider match strategy, driver practice, pit preparation, and teamwork. Our goal is to make both the robot and the team ready for competition.

OUR PROCESS

Ideas become engineering.

STEP 01

Understand the Challenge

Review the game rules, identify objectives, and plan a strategy.

STEP 02

Design the Solution

Sketch ideas, compare mechanisms, and create CAD models where useful.

STEP 03

Build and Program

Assemble the robot, develop its software, and test individual systems.

STEP 04

Improve and Compete

Use practice runs and competition results to find the next improvement.

OFFICIAL 501(c)(3) NONPROFIT

Lobster Machine is backed by Horseshoe Club, an official 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations made to our team go through Horseshoe Club, which means supporters give through a registered nonprofit rather than directly to individual students. Your gift may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please check with your own tax advisor.
